How it works

Set it up in 5 minutes.
Kids stay busy for hours.

Parents are in full control. Kids get a personalized world they actually want to return to every single day.

1

Create your child's profile

Enter their name, age group, preferred language, and learning goals. Takes under 2 minutes.

2

Pick their world & subjects

Choose from 8 adventure themes including Construction, Space, Ocean, and more, then toggle on the subjects you want covered.

3

Set the daily schedule

Add unlock times, screen limits, and custom tasks. Each quest appears at the right time, so there's no negotiating.

4

Kids earn XP & level up

Completing quests earns XP, badges, and unlocks new content. Progress reports come straight to you weekly.
